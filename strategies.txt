# STRATEGIES USED


#1 FLAT BETTOR

Bet the same amount, regardless of a win or lose, subject to table limits and available bankroll.
It provides a simple control or baseline for comparing the other betting methods.

Limitation: Flat betting doesn't respond to favorable cards or changing probabilities.
It also doesn't eliminate the casino's advantage.



#2 HALF KELLY

Bet more when your estimated probability advantage is higher, but adjust your bet according to how much money you have.
Uses an estimated player advantage based on the true count to calculate a bankroll-based wager, then takes half of that calculated
Kelly stake. 

In this system:
  estimated_edge = max(0, 0.005 * true_count)
  full_kelly_stake = bankroll * estimated_edge / 1.3
  half_kelly_stake = 0.5 * full_kelly_stake

Advantage: This method adjusts bet size with the estimated advantage and bankroll. (unlike martingale)



#3 HI-LO SPREAD 

Track the cards that have already appeared to estimate whether the remaining deck is favorable.

In this system we assign the values:
  +1: 2-6 (low)
   0: 7-9 (neutral)
  -1: 10, face cards (high)

The running count is the sum of the values assigned to the cards dealt
The true value = running count/decks remaining

he Hi-Lo spread bot bets one base unit below its true-count threshold (default +1). 
At or above the threshold, it scales the number of units with the rounded true count, up to a default maximum of 8 units.



#4 BASIC STRATEGY

Use mathematical decision tables to determine which action has the best expected result for the player's hand.
  -> entails playing cards instead of adjusting bet money

Uses predefined decision tables for hard hands, soft hands, and pairs to recommend a move based on 
the player's cards and the dealer's visible card.
It also considers which actions are legal under the table rules, including when splitting, surrendering, or doubling is permitted.
The goal is to minimize expected loss for the configured rules rather than to predict the next card.

Wagers a fixed base unit and follows these recommendations. 
  ->Because Flat Bettor also inherits the same basic-strategy playing decisions, these two bots can behave similarly.



#5 MARTINGALE
The Martingale bot increases its next bet after a losing round, generally by doubling for every consecutive loss. 

In this implementation:
  wager = base unit * 2^(current loss streak)

Table limits and the remaining bankroll may further cap these bets. 
Martingale changes wager size based on past outcomes, not on card composition or an improved player advantage. I
t can lose money very quickly during a losing streak and does not remove the house edge.
